# RLFSP Logframe (fictional programme)

**Resilient Livelihoods & Food Security Programme (RLFSP)** is a fictional 3-year (Mar 2023 - Feb 2026), donor-funded smallholder programme
in four districts (Northvale, Eastmere, Southridge, Westbrook) delivered by two implementing partners (Partner A: agronomy; Partner B: VSLA and market linkages).
Partners overlap in Eastmere and Southridge. The MEL team runs a household panel survey (1,200 households; baseline Apr-May 2023, midline Apr-May 2024,
endline Apr-May 2025, collected ahead of close-out to inform final-year decisions) with 24 enumerators, plus routine partner monitoring.

| Level | Statement | Indicator | PIRS | Source | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goal | Improved food security and resilience of smallholder households | FCS (% acceptable); rCSI; HHS | 01, 02, 03 | Household panel survey | Baseline / midline / endline |
| Outcome 1 | Households adopt improved agronomic practices | % HHs adopting >=2 improved practices | 04 | Household panel survey | Annual |
| Outcome 2 | Increased productivity of staple crops | Maize yield (kg/ha) | 05 | Household panel survey (farmer-reported) | Annual |
| Outcome 3 | Increased household income and savings | Monthly household income (USD); VSLA savings (USD) | 06 | Household panel survey | Annual |
| Output 1 | Farmers trained in improved practices | # farmers trained (unique, by sex and age) | 07 | Partner training registers | Quarterly |
| Output 2 | Households receive agricultural inputs | # households receiving inputs (post-distribution monitoring) | 08 | Partner PDM | Quarterly |

## Assumptions and risks (relevant to data quality)
- Partners use separate registers with no shared beneficiary ID (risk: double counting).
- Enumerators are paid per completed interview (risk: fabrication under target pressure).
- Poorer and more mobile households are harder to re-contact (risk: differential attrition).
- Income questions are sensitive (risk: non-random missingness).

*Note: the synthetic generator implements Outcomes 1-3 and Output 1 in full. Output 2 (inputs / PDM) is documented here but not generated in v0.1 (see roadmap in README).*
